Greater Noida West's Decoory Interiors Honoured with NEFOMA's “Certificate of Excellence”

Recognition for quality, reliability and timely project delivery.

GREATER NOIDA WEST — Decoory Interiors

Homegrown interior design company in Greater Noida, Decoory Interiors has been honoured with the “Certificate of Excellence” by the Noida Extension Flat Owners & Members Association (NEFOMA) for its quality, creativity, technical expertise, reliability and commitment to timely project delivery.

At a ceremony held in Greater Noida West, NEFOMA President Annu Khan presented the honour to the Decoory Interiors team. He appreciated the company's focus on quality, transparency, timely execution and customer satisfaction, noting that customer trust is the foundation of any successful organisation.

The recognition adds to Decoory Interiors' growing list of honours. The company has previously been recognised as a “Best Interior Company” by media organisations and publications including Dainik Bhaskar, News Today and Live Today.

From Local Roots to a Growing Interior Brand

Decoory Interiors was formally established in 2017 in Delhi-NCR. Before establishing the brand, its director Krishna Nand had spent nearly a decade building a reputation in the interior sector through referrals, quality workmanship and customer trust.

Starting with smaller projects and a few carpenter teams, the company gradually evolved into a professionally structured interior design and execution company, offering end-to-end solutions from design and planning to execution and project handover.

Today, Decoory Interiors caters to homeowners seeking a reliable interior designer in Greater Noida West and interior designer in Noida Extension, with a focus on personalised design, functionality and quality execution.

Quality-Driven Interior Solutions

Decoory Interiors places strong emphasis on branded raw materials, experienced in-house interior designers, trained technical professionals and skilled execution teams. Designs are customised according to the client's lifestyle, requirements, budget and available space.

The company is ISO 9001:2015 quality management certified and has also earned a 5-star Google rating.

Its services include home interior design, full home interiors, modular kitchen design, furniture, storage solutions, space planning, lighting and false ceilings. It also provides customised 2BHK interior design and 3BHK interior design solutions.

Rather than limiting its role to design, the company follows an integrated approach covering planning, material selection, execution, finishing and final handover.

Why Choose Decoory Interiors?

For homeowners looking for a dependable interior design company in Greater Noida, Decoory Interiors combines professional design expertise with quality materials, trained execution teams and end-to-end project management.

Its focus on transparent communication, quality workmanship and timely project delivery is designed to give homeowners a smoother interior experience — from the initial design concept to final handover.

This integrated approach has helped the company strengthen its presence in the rapidly developing residential markets of Greater Noida West and Noida Extension.

Expanding Across North India

Headquartered at Gaur City Centre, Greater Noida West, Decoory Interiors has expanded its presence with branches in Moti Nagar, New Delhi, Manali and Rishikesh.

The company is now working on a franchise-led expansion roadmap to extend its interior design and execution services to more cities across North India, while maintaining its focus on quality and customer service.
