# Decoory Interiors Brand And Conversion Direction

## Logo Concept

The current concept uses a restrained `DI` monogram inside a fine champagne-gold square, paired with an editorial serif wordmark and wide-tracked `INTERIORS` descriptor. It is designed to feel premium, residential, calm and detail-led rather than decorative.

## Brand Identity

- Palette: matte black, deep espresso, warm stone, champagne gold, ivory.
- Mood: cinematic, quiet luxury, gallery-like spacing, high contrast typography.
- Voice: short, confident, aspirational, execution-focused.
- Visual cues: warm living rooms, modular kitchens, bedrooms, wardrobes, Italian marble, wooden panels, custom joinery and hotel-level lighting.

## Typography Suggestions

- Display: Cormorant Garamond, Canela, Ivy Mode, Editorial New, or Freight Display.
- Sans: Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, Satoshi, Suisse Int'l, or Avenir Next.
- Usage: oversized serif headlines, small uppercase sans-serif labels, concise body copy.

## Homepage Copywriting System

- Hero: "Designing Spaces That Define Luxury"
- Trust: "Trusted By Hundreds Of Premium Homeowners"
- Projects: "Editorial interiors built for modern luxury living"
- About: "We do not just design interiors. We create experiences that elevate everyday living."
- CTA: "Your Dream Space Starts Here"

## Image Placement

- Hero: cinematic luxury living room or modular kitchen with warm lighting and a lived-in aspirational home mood.
- Projects: masonry cards with room-specific imagery and style tags.
- About: founder or studio direction visual beside emotional brand philosophy.
- CTA: darker warm lounge backdrop to keep form legible and premium.
- Footer: compact Instagram-style preview grid for social credibility.

## Animation Ideas

- Hero image fade and subtle scale for cinematic residential lifestyle movement.
- Scroll-triggered section reveals with GSAP.
- Framer Motion filter transitions and gallery modal.
- Hover zoom on project cards.
- Premium edge tab for "Get Quote" to keep conversion available without blocking content.

## Lead Generation Strategy

- Primary CTA appears above the fold, in navbar, hero, services, and final CTA.
- WhatsApp link is sticky on desktop and paired with call action on mobile.
- Budget and property selectors qualify high-ticket leads early.
- Trust signals appear immediately after hero to reduce paid-traffic bounce.
- Consultation urgency is included without sounding cheap: "Limited consultation slots available this month."
